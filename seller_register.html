Service Provider Details

Service Licences & Certificates (Optional)

You may upload your service provision licences, certificates, training certificates, or other relevant professional documents. These documents are not mandatory, but verified documents may give you an added advantage when LEOGO reviews and recommends service providers to customers.
Accepted: PDF, JPG, JPEG or PNG. Maximum 5 MB per document. All uploads are optional.

Transport & Delivery Details

Driving & Vehicle Documents (Optional)

You may upload your driving licence and any other relevant transport or vehicle documents. These documents are not mandatory, but verified documents may give you an added advantage when LEOGO reviews and recommends transport and delivery providers to customers.
Accepted: PDF, JPG, JPEG or PNG. Maximum 5 MB per document. All uploads are optional.
Your vehicle will be submitted with Pending approval. LEOGO can verify the vehicle before it is made available for customer requests.

Settlement Account

LEOGO collects customer payments and uses this information to pay your approved earnings. At least one payout account is required. You may provide more than one option. Your settlement details are private and are only used for LEOGO payouts and administration.
